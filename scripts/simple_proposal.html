New Features to Add to the Church App

A Simple Everyday Guide for Review & Planning

Notice: This document lists only 100% new features to build next. Modules already completed (such as Sunday attendance, Paystack MoMo giving, bulk SMS, visitor follow-up, children's check-in, and offline Bibles) have been excluded.

1. Better Pastoral Care & Stopping Members from Leaving

  • "Missing Member" Warning Alerts: If a member misses 3 Sundays in a row, the app automatically flags their name so the welfare team or cell leader can call or visit before they stop coming completely.
  • Pastoral Visit & Care Tracker: A simple shared list to track who is sick in the hospital, who is bereaved, or who needs counseling, showing which pastor is assigned and what help has been given.
  • Life-Event Milestones: Record important spiritual moments: Water Baptism, Holy Ghost Baptism, Baby Dedications, and New Believers Class graduation.
  • Birthday & Wedding Anniversary Wishes: Automatically sends a warm text message signed with the Senior Pastor's name on their special day.
  • Discipleship Ladder: A visual step-by-step progress tracker showing a person moving from First-Time Visitor → New Convert → Water Baptized → Worker → Cell Leader.

2. Ghana-Style Church Giving & Money Management

  • Giving for People Without Smartphones: Clear on-screen instructions showing the church MoMo Merchant Number or USSD code (like *xxx#) for older members who use button phones.
  • Recording Cash & Direct Bank Transfers: An easy page to record Sunday physical cash envelopes, cheques, and direct bank transfers with reference numbers so 100% of giving is accounted for in one place.
  • Ghana Church Giving Categories: Built-in buttons for funds common in Ghanaian churches: Tithe, First Fruit, Thanksgiving, Building Fund, Welfare Seed, Church Anniversary, and Annual Harvest.
  • Fundraising Progress Thermometer: A visual progress bar showing how close the church is to buying a new bus, building a cathedral, or meeting annual harvest targets.
  • Bank Account Reconciliation in Cedis (GHS): An automated report that matches Mobile Money payouts against what actually entered the church's bank account.
  • Official Tax & Donation Receipts: Downloadable PDF donation receipts for registered charities, companies, or church members living abroad.

3. Smooth Sunday Services & Facility Operations

  • Pulpit & Stage Countdown Timers: A digital stage clock for the praise team, pastor, and sound crew showing remaining minutes for Worship, Sermon, Offering, and Closing Prayer.
  • Auditorium Overflow Warnings: Alerts ushers when the main hall is 85% or 95% full so they can open the overflow tent or release extra chairs in time.
  • Equipment & Instrument Register: Keep track of church property (microphones, soundboards, instruments, projectors, plastic chairs, bus keys) and see who borrowed what and when it was returned.
  • Meeting Room Reservations: A booking calendar for the chapel, board room, and counseling offices so departments never fight over rooms.
  • Meeting Minutes & Action Items: Storing Church Board or Deacon minutes with clear deadlines and names assigned to each task.
  • Budget vs. Actual Spending: A simple report showing what each department was budgeted to spend versus what was actually spent.

4. Cultural Customs & Denominational Growth

  • Ceremonies & Bereavement Support: Special coordination for One-Week Observances, Funerals, and Outdoorings / Naming Ceremonies, including collecting voluntary support funds for the family.
  • Branch-to-Headquarters Remittances: Automatically calculates the percentage of tithes or offerings that satellite branches must send to HQ every week, with summaries for Regional Overseers.
  • Denomination Presets: Pre-configured wording and structures for Pentecostal / Charismatic (districts, cells), Methodist / Presbyterian / Catholic (dioceses, circuits, societies, parishes), and Apostolic churches.
  • Ghana Card Volunteer Safety Check: An optional Ghana Card identity verification for children's church teachers to protect kids.
  • Smart Message Routing: When a member texts back about prayer or grief, the message is automatically routed to the right team (prayer team, welfare, or pastor).
  • Online Attendance & Diaspora Giving: Track attendance on YouTube/Facebook live streams and accept card giving from members abroad in USD, EUR, and GBP.

5. The Top 4 Features to Build First (Quick Wins)

Feature Why Build This First Effort
1. Missing-Member Alerts Stops people from quietly slipping out of church by using attendance numbers we already record. Quick & Low Effort
2. Manual MoMo & Cash Entry Captures non-card giving so the church finance committee has a 100% complete picture. Quick & Low Effort
3. Harvest & Building Thermometer Directly motivates members and drives project fundraising during annual harvest season. Quick & Low Effort
4. Branch-to-HQ Remittance Tracking Huge selling point that allows you to sell the system to large denominations with 10+ branches. Medium Effort
