ChMS Feature Expansion Roadmap (New Proposed Modules)

Document Purpose: A curated proposal of 100% new, unbuilt features to expand the Church Management System into pastoral care, church operations, and localized Ghanaian church workflows.

(Note: Existing built modules—such as offline usher attendance, Paystack MoMo giving, bulk SMS, drip follow-up, children's check-in, multi-branch RLS, and the multilingual Bible—are excluded from this proposal).

Pillar 1: Pastoral Care & Member Retention

  • "Missing Member" Automated Welfare Alerts:
  • Automatically scans attendance data and flags any member who has missed 3+ consecutive Sundays.
  • Instantly queues a home-visitation or phone-call task for the welfare team or cell leader before members drift away.
  • Pastoral Care Case Tracker:
  • Centralized management to log, assign, and track hospital visits, bereavement cases, pastoral counseling, and crisis welfare assistance.
  • Status tracking (New, In Progress, Resolved) with designated minister assignments.
  • Spiritual Milestones & Life-Event Tracking:
  • Record and celebrate key church milestones: Water Baptism, Holy Ghost Baptism, Child Dedications, New Believers Class completion, and Marriages.
  • Automated Pastor-Signed Celebrations:
  • Automatic birthday and wedding anniversary SMS messages signed with the Senior Pastor's name.
  • Discipleship Pathway Progression Funnel:
  • Visual spiritual pathway tracking member growth:

First-Timer ➔ New Believers Class ➔ Baptism ➔ Ministry Worker ➔ Cell Leader.

Pillar 2: Ghana Market Fit & Localized Financial Workflows

  • Offline & Non-Smartphone Giving Instructions:
  • Dedicated on-screen and printed guides showing church MoMo Merchant IDs and USSD codes (*xxx#) for members using non-smart feature phones.
  • Manual Cash, Cheque & MoMo-Transfer Ledger:
  • Direct data entry for bank deposits, cheques, and direct MoMo transfers with reference numbers so the ledger captures 100% of giving outside the payment gateway.
  • Ghanaian Giving Categories:
  • Built-in funds common in Ghana: Tithe, First Fruit, Thanksgiving, Building Fund, Welfare Seed, Church Anniversary, Annual Harvest, and Prophetic Seed.
  • Annual Harvest & Project Fundraising Thermometers:
  • Visual fundraising progress bars with target amounts and countdown dates for cathedral expansions, bus purchases, and harvest thanksgivings.
  • Bank Reconciliation in GHS:
  • Automated matching reports comparing Paystack Mobile Money settlements against actual bank account deposits.
  • Tax-Exemption & Charity Certificates:
  • Formal annual contribution certificates for registered charitable trusts, businesses, and diaspora tax filings.

Pillar 3: Sunday Sanctuary & Operations Flow

  • Stage Flow & Run-of-Show Timers:
  • Live digital countdown schedule for the stage and pulpit: Praise & Worship, Announcements, Sermon, Offering, and Benediction.
  • Auditorium Overflow & Overcapacity Alerts:
  • Real-time warnings when main sanctuary seating reaches 85% or 95% capacity, prompting protocol to open overflow halls or release auxiliary seating.
  • Asset & Equipment Inventory Register:
  • Track church assets (microphones, sound consoles, instruments, projectors, plastic chairs, buses) with digital checkout, return, and maintenance logs.
  • Facility & Room Booking Calendar:
  • Reservation system for church conference rooms, chapel, youth hall, and counseling offices to prevent double-booking.
  • Leadership Meeting Minutes & Action Tracker:
  • Secure repository for Church Council, Deacon Board, and Committee meeting minutes with assigned action-item deadlines.
  • Annual Budget vs. Actuals Reporting:
  • Real-time variance reports comparing projected department budgets against actual income and expenditures.

Pillar 4: Cultural, Denominational & Governance Fit

  • Ghanaian Ceremonies & Bereavement Support:
  • Specialized event workflows for One-Week Observances, Funerals, and Outdoorings/Naming Ceremonies, including voluntary family donation collections.
  • Denominational Hierarchy & Remittance Tracking:
  • Automated weekly calculation and tracking of branch-to-HQ percentage remittances (common across Ghanaian denominations).
  • Multi-level rollups for District, Area, and Regional Overseers.
  • Denominational Structure Presets:
  • Ready-made terminology and hierarchy presets for:
  • Pentecostal & Charismatic (Assemblies of God, Church of Pentecost, Lighthouse, Action Chapel).
  • Mainline & Historic (Methodist, Presbyterian, Catholic: Dioceses, Circuits, Societies, Parishes).
  • Apostolic & Independent Ministries.
  • Ghana Card Volunteer Verification:
  • Optional Ghana Card validation check for volunteers working in Children's Church without storing sensitive raw ID numbers.
  • Two-Way SMS & WhatsApp Department Routing:
  • Inbound member text replies and WhatsApp messages automatically routed to the right ministry desk (Prayer team, Welfare, Pastor).
  • Hybrid Service Attendance & Diaspora Giving:
  • Integration with Facebook and YouTube live streams for online attendance counts, plus international card giving in GHS, USD, EUR, and GBP.
