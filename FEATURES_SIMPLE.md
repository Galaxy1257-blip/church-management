# Church Management System (ChMS) — Simple Everyday Guide

An easy-to-understand breakdown of what this app does, how it helps your church grow, and why it makes running services effortless.

---

## 1. Keeping Track of Members and Families

* **All Your Members in One Place**: Keep every member's name, phone number, birthday, hometown address, and Ghana digital address organized.
* **Know Where People Are in Their Journey**: Easily see who is a first-time visitor, a new convert, an active member, or someone who has stopped coming so you can reach out.
* **Family Grouping**: Connect husbands, wives, and children together under one household so you know who belongs to which family.
* **Import Existing Lists with 1 Click**: If you already have your members in an Excel or CSV file, you can upload it in seconds without typing names one by one.

---

## 2. Sunday Headcount (Works Even With Zero Internet!)

* **No Internet? No Problem**: Church auditoriums often have poor mobile network. Ushers can count men, women, children, and visitors on their phones with **zero Wi-Fi or data**.
* **Automatic Cloud Sync**: As soon as the usher walks outside or reconnects to data, all headcount numbers save to the system automatically.
* **Quick Check-In**: Mark members present with a quick name search or by scanning a personal QR code on their phone.
* **Growth Reports**: See beautiful graphs showing whether attendance is growing week after week.

---

## 3. Tithes, Offerings & Mobile Money (MoMo)

* **Pay with Ghana Mobile Money & Cards**: Members can give tithes and offerings directly using **MTN Mobile Money, Telecel Cash, AT Money, or Visa/Mastercard**.
* **Easy Giving Page (`/give`)**: A simple public link you can post on Facebook, WhatsApp, or YouTube during live streams. Members don't even need an account to give.
* **Choose What You're Giving Towards**: Members can specify if money is for Tithe, General Offering, Building Project, Welfare, or Pastor's Appreciation.
* **Zero Guesswork Fees**: Members can choose to cover the small MoMo transaction fee so the church gets the exact full amount.
* **Instant SMS Receipts**: The moment a payment goes through, the giver receives an instant thank-you text message acknowledging their gift.

---

## 4. Church Money, Cash Counting & Expenses

* **Safe Sunday Cash Counting**: Two people count the physical cash and cheques together, sign off on the total, and a supervisor approves it before it is banked.
* **Fund Ledgers**: Clearly see how much money is sitting in the Building Account, the Welfare Account, and the Main Church Account.
* **Pledges & Projects**: Set a target for a new bus or building project, track who pledged what, and send polite reminders to help them fulfill their pledge.
* **Expense Requests & Approvals**: Workers can request funds for church supplies or repairs, attach photos of receipts, and leaders can approve or reject them on their phone.

---

## 5. Bulk SMS Messaging

* **Send Texts with Your Church Name**: Texts arrive on members' phones showing your official church name (e.g. *“ICGC-Grace”* or *“COP-Central”*) instead of an unknown number.
* **Birthday & Anniversary Wishes**: Automatically wish members happy birthday on their special day.
* **Targeted Messages**: Send a text only to Men's Ministry, only to Youth, or to the entire church with one click.
* **Pre-written Templates**: Save time with ready-made templates for Sunday service reminders, fastings, and emergency announcements.
* **Respects Privacy**: If someone no longer wishes to receive texts, they can simply reply `STOP` and the system automatically stops texting them.

---

## 6. Automatic Follow-Up for First-Time Visitors

* **Never Lose a Soul**: When a visitor fills their contact slip, the system starts a warm, automatic follow-up sequence.
* **Gentle Check-ins Over Time**:
  * *Sunday afternoon*: A warm text thanking them for worshipping today.
  * *Wednesday*: A friendly mid-week check-in and prayer offer.
  * *Saturday*: An invitation to tomorrow's service with sermon topic.
* **Sleeps at Night**: Never texts people late at night—holds messages until the morning so visitors feel respected.
* **Smart Detection**: If a visitor replies back to a text, the robot immediately stops and alerts a pastor or follow-up worker to take over personal care.

---

## 7. Pastoral Care, Prayer Wall & Welfare Support

* **Private Counseling Notes**: Pastors can write counseling notes that are completely locked and hidden from ordinary staff and volunteers.
* **Prayer Wall**: Members can submit prayer requests from their phones. Pastors can mark them as public for the prayer team or private for pastors only.
* **Welfare & Helping Hands**: Keep track of benevolence requests, food or financial support given to members in need, ensuring help goes where it is needed most.

---

## 8. Safe Children’s Church Check-In

* **Matching Security Tickets**: When a parent drops off their child, both parent and child get matching security codes.
* **Safe Pickup**: Nobody can pick up a child after church unless their security code matches.
* **Allergy & Medical Alerts**: Prints or displays critical alerts (e.g., *“No peanuts”* or *“Asthmatic”*) so teachers know how to care for every child.

---

## 9. Home Cells & Small Groups

* **Cell Fellowship Tracking**: Divide the church into neighborhood cells or home fellowships.
* **Cell Leaders in Control**: Cell leaders can mark weekly meeting attendance and submit reports straight from their smartphone.

---

## 10. Volunteer Duty Rosters (Choir, Ushers, Media)

* **Organized Schedules**: Schedule who is on duty for Sunday (ushers, praise team, sound engineers, children's teachers).
* **Easy Confirmations**: Volunteers get a text and reply **YES** to confirm or **NO** if they cannot make it.
* **No Double Booking**: The app warns you if you accidentally schedule the same person to sing and usher at the same service.
* **Quick Backups**: If someone declines, the app suggests available backups with one tap.

---

## 11. Church Events, RSVPs & Conferences

* **Event Calendars**: Post upcoming revivals, all-night vigils, youth camps, and leadership seminars.
* **Free or Paid Registration**: Let attendees RSVP, book bus seats, or pay conference registration fees online.

---

## 12. Worship Music & Sunday Setlists

* **Song & Lyric Database**: Keep all your praise and worship lyrics, chords, and song keys organized in one place.
* **Sunday Setlists**: The worship leader can build the Sunday song lineup in advance so instrumentalists and media projection teams are fully prepared.

---

## 13. Built-In Ghana Bibles (Works 100% Offline)

* **No Data Needed to Read Scripture**: Read God's Word anywhere, even without an internet connection.
* **Available in Local Languages**:
  * **English**: King James Version (KJV) and American Standard Version (ASV).
  * **Akuapem Twi**: Full Old & New Testament with proper local alphabets (`ɔ`, `ɛ`).
  * **Asante Twi**: Complete 66 books, all 1,189 chapters and 31,102 verses.
  * **Fante**: Complete Old and New Testament.
  * **Ga**: Complete Old and New Testament.
  * **Ewe**: Complete local translation.
* **Instant Search & Copy**: Search for any word or phrase and copy verses into sermons or church social media posts.

---

## 14. Member Self-Service Portal & WhatsApp Flyers

* **Member Portal (`/portal`)**: Members can log in securely using their phone number to:
  * Check their past donations and download an annual giving statement.
  * Check their assigned volunteer duty dates.
  * Submit prayer requests.
* **Flyer Generator (`/flyers`)**: Create beautiful, professional service announcement graphics with your church logo to post on WhatsApp status and Instagram in seconds.

---

## 15. Safe, Private & Multi-Branch Ready

* **Bank-Grade Data Privacy**: Your church data belongs strictly to your church. No other church or outsider can ever see your numbers.
* **Multi-Branch Support**: If your church has multiple branches (e.g., Accra, Kumasi, Takoradi), each branch can run its own services while headquarters sees the combined picture.
* **Controlled Access**: Give ushers access only to attendance, accountants access only to money, and pastors access to counseling.
